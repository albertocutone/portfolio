### Meta Reality Labs (Contractor on behalf of Qualitest), Zurich

- id: meta
- duration: Dec 2022 - Ongoing
- role: Computer Vision SW Engineer
- keywords: C++ | Python | Computer Vision | Mixed Reality | OpenXR | Unity | PyTorch | CI/CD | BPF


#### Situation

At Meta Reality Labs, I am part of the team working on mixed reality (MR) software for virtual and augmented reality (VR/AR) head-mounted devices (HMDs). The team focuses on enhancing computer vision (CV) and MR features on Oculus Quest devices, optimizing performance, and automating key processes to improve the quality of MR applications.


#### Task & Action

- Developed and improved computer vision algorithms using PyTorch to enhance their accuracy and performance.

- Integrated Berkeley Packet Filter (BPF) into our testing frameworks to monitor CPU, GPU, and memory usage, leading to better performance insights and more stable MR applications.

- Automated and refined key aspects of Passthrough applications within Unity, improving both development speed and the end-user experience.


#### Result

- Streamlined MR quality benchmarking and reduced manual tracking efforts, leading to faster and more reliable iterations.

- Enhanced system stability and resource management through detailed performance monitoring, optimizing MR applications on HMDs.

- Accelerated development of MR features, improving both the efficiency of Passthrough application deployment and the overall user experience.


#### Reflection

This role has sharpened my skills in software development, computer vision, and automation, while contributing to innovative MR products on cutting-edge HMD technology.

## Academic experience

### MASTER THESIS: Cryo-Submarine project, CERN (Geneva)

- id: cern
- duration: 6 months
- role: SW/HW Engineer, Python, Linux
- keywords: Python | Linux | Altium Designer | camera systems | robotics


#### Situation

CERN, the world’s leading laboratory for Particle Physics, was conducting neutrino detection experiments that required precise measurements inside large cryostats filled with liquid Argon at -200°C. A cryogenic robot was needed to perform these measurements under those extreme conditions..


#### Task & Action

- Designed the architecture for the cryogenic robot, ensuring reliability under low-temperature conditions.

- Tested and characterized electronic components in Liquid Nitrogen (-200°C) to ensure they could operate in extreme environments.

- Developed a cryogenic camera system using a Raspberry Pi, sensors, and a heating mechanism to withstand extreme cold.

- Wrote Python scripts to control and manage system components.

- As a side project, I was entitled to design and develop an electronic board for measuring Liquid Argon levels in a cryostat, overseeing its production and testing.


#### Result

- Built a functional cryogenic camera system that operated successfully at -200°C.

- Developed an electronic board for Liquid Argon measurement, which performed exceptionally well and was replicated for use in the experiment.

- Contributed reliable solutions that enhanced the accuracy and efficiency of neutrino detection experiments at CERN.


#### Reflection

My experience at CERN gave me hands-on expertise in hardware-software integration, cryogenic systems, and embedded electronics in extreme environments.

### BACHELOR THESIS: C++ image processing SW, Policlinic Umberto I (Rome)

- id: cardiac
- company: Policlinico Umberto I
- emoji: 🫀
- location: Rome
- projects: cardiac
- duration: 5 months
- role: SW Engineer
- keywords: C++ | Matlab | Qt Designer


#### Situation

During my bachelor thesis at Policlinic Umberto I in Rome, I developed a C++ image processing software to assist cardiologists in diagnosing cardiac tissue anomalies, particularly for detecting heart conditions such as necrosis.


#### Task & Action

- Implemented a C++ algorithm to analyze DICOM images from Cardiac Magnetic Resonance (CMR) scans, focusing on movement compensation to highlight anomalies.

- Developed a graphical user interface (GUI) using Qt Creator IDE to provide an intuitive experience for medical professionals.

- Designed and optimized an algorithm to equalize breath and heart movement, enabling better visualization of cardiac tissue anomalies.

- Used image registration and data processing techniques to combine and enhance visual data for accurate diagnosis.


#### Result

- Successfully developed a tool that generates a color-coded map, aiding doctors in diagnosing heart attacks and visualizing tissue abnormalities.

- Delivered a working prototype that demonstrated the practical application of image processing in medical diagnostics.


#### Reflection

This project strengthened my skills in C++, medical imaging, and algorithm optimization, and deepened my understanding of real-world applications of software in healthcare.
